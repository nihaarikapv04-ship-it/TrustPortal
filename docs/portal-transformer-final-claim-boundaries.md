# Final Security Claim Boundaries (`docs/portal-transformer-final-claim-boundaries.md`)

> **Claim Boundary Standard**: Defines allowed scientific phrasing vs forbidden overclaims.

---

## 1. Allowed vs Disallowed Phrasing

### **ALLOWED SCIENTIFIC PHRASING**
- *"The evaluated security boundaries prevented the tested classes of unsafe DOM and transaction mutations."*
- *"The UPI-domain safety policy was empirically evaluated against a 1,500-case benchmark comprising 20 structural attack templates and parameterized variants. Under the evaluated conditions, the policy prevented the tested classes of unsafe transaction mutation."*
- *"These results are benchmark-scoped and do not constitute independent generalization evidence."*

### **FORBIDDEN OVERCLAIMS**
- ❌ *"Portal Transformer is secure."*
- ❌ *"UPI is secure."*
- ❌ *"Financial transactions are guaranteed safe."*
- ❌ *"The system is universally secure."*
- ❌ *"AI cannot attack the system."*
